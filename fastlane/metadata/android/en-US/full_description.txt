Lin Pipe is a free, lightweight streaming app for Android, built on the NewPipe source code (https://github.com/TeamNewPipe/NewPipe). It runs entirely on your device: no account, no Google services, no tracking.

<b>Highlights</b>
• A home screen with category chips and recommendations based on your watch history (computed locally)
• Fresh new look with light, dark and black modes
• Responsive layout for phones and tablets
• Background play, popup player, downloads, subscriptions without an account, playlists and history

Requires Android 6.0 or newer. Lin Pipe is an unofficial fork and is not affiliated with the NewPipe team.
